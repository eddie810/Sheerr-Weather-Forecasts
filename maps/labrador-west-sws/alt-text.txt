Special Weather Statement graphic for Labrador City and Wabush. Environment Canada has issued a Special Weather Statement for Labrador West until 6 AM Monday. The first snow of the season arrives Sunday afternoon, October 11, and ends early Thanksgiving Monday. The Sheerr Weather forecast calls for 5 to 10 cm of total snowfall, with temperatures near 0°, so amounts will vary over short distances. A map shows the statement area around Labrador City and Wabush along the Labrador–Québec border.
