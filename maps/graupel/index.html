What is graupel?

Those soft white pellets that bounce off the deck and crumble in your hand. It's not snow, not hail and not ice pellets: it's a snowflake that got coated in frozen cloud droplets.

How it formsInside a cold shower cloud
1Snowflake

Ice crystals grow into a snowflake high in the cloud, where it's well below freezing.

2Riming

The flake falls through supercooled droplets: liquid water below 0°C. They freeze on contact and coat it in rime.

3Graupel

Coated until the flake's shape disappears: a soft, white, opaque pellet, about 2 to 5 mm across.

In NL:common in fall and spring snow showers and squalls. Environment Canada reports call it "snow pellets".
