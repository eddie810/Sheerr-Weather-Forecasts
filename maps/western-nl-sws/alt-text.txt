Special Weather Statement graphic for western and northern Newfoundland. Environment Canada has issued a Special Weather Statement for the Great Northern Peninsula, including Parson's Pond–Hawke's Bay, Gros Morne and Green Bay–White Bay, in effect until 3:30 PM Monday. The first notable snowfall of the season is expected on higher terrain from late Sunday evening into Monday afternoon. The Sheerr Weather forecast calls for 2 to 10 cm above about 150 metres, with 15 cm or more possible on the Long Range plateau. The coast and valleys will see mostly rain or a mix of rain and wet snow. A map shades the statement area.
